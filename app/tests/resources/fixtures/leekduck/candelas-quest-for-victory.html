GO Pass

GO Pass and GO Pass Deluxe

A GO Pass is a free, limited-time progression track available during a specified period. By collecting GO Points, you can increase your rank to earn rewards.

Trainers will automatically receive GO Pass: Candela’s Quest for Victory on Tuesday, June 9, at 10:00 a.m. local time. You can collect GO Points and rank up to get additional rewards through Monday, June 15, at 8:00 p.m. local time.

Additionally, from Saturday, June 13, at 12:00 a.m. to Monday, June 15, at 7:59 p.m. local time, there’s no daily limit for how many GO Points you can earn!

For US$4.99*, Trainers can upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe, a paid version of the GO Pass that offers upgraded rewards and faster progression.

For US$6.99*, Trainers can upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe + 6 Ranks to also automatically earn enough GO Points to reach Rank 7.

While progressing through the GO Pass Deluxe, you’ll be able to claim all of the rewards from both the free GO Pass and GO Pass Deluxe. You can upgrade to a GO Pass Deluxe at any time and still collect rewards from previously unlocked ranks.

Rewards unlocked in the GO Pass will expire on Wednesday, June 17, at 7:59 p.m. local time, so be sure to claim your rewards before they’re gone!

*All prices listed are in USD or the equivalent pricing tier in your local currency.

Watch for additional bonus tasks for your GO Pass: Candela’s Quest for Victory during the event!

Featured Pokemon and Rewards

Complete Pass Tasks to rank up your GO Pass to earn the following rewards.

  • Encounters with Flareon, Rockruff, Ponyta wearing a Candela-themed accessory with a background inspired by Team Valor, and more!

Trainers who upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe can also earn the following rewards.

  • Additional encounters with Ponyta wearing a Candela-themed accessory with a background inspired by Team Valor
  • 6 additional Premium Battle Passes
  • And even more goodies!

Major Milestone Bonuses

Reach Major Milestones on your GO Pass to unlock the following bonuses.

Tier 1 Bonus Starting at Rank 10

Increased Attack bonus from friends in raids

Tier 2 Bonus Starting at Rank 20

1.5x Raid Stardust

Upgrade to the GO Pass Deluxe to get the following boosted bonuses.

Tier 2 Bonus Starting at Rank 20

Receive up to two Raid Passes by spinning Photo Discs at Gyms

How to Earn Points

Earn points to increase your rank and unlock rewards. You can earn points by completing Pass Tasks and bonus tasks during the event.

Any rewards you earn for completing a Pass Task will be awarded as soon as you finish the task.

Trainers can earn up to 500 extra points daily by completing certain tasks.

  • Win a raid - 100 PTS
  • Hatch an Egg - 40 PTS
  • Catch a Pokémon - 5 PTS

In addition, there will be a Daily Task worth 100 PTS: Power up Pokémon 10 times.

Daily Tasks reset each day and may change throughout the event.

Sales

Pokémon GO Web Store

The GO Pass Deluxe, the paid version of the GO Pass, will be available on the Pokemon GO Web Store soon. Your GO Pass Deluxe purchased via the Pokemon GO Web Store will activate as soon as the GO Pass begins.

Trainers who upgrade to GO Pass Deluxe: Candela’s Quest for Victory or GO Pass Deluxe: Candela’s Quest for Victory + 6 Ranks on the Pokemon GO Web Store will receive 10 Ultra Balls, five Max Revives, one Premium Battle Pass, and five Max Potions as a gift with purchase.

Or stock up on the essentials alongside your upgrade with the web-exclusive GO Pass Deluxe: Candela’s Quest for Victory + 6 Ranks Ultra Box which includes 20 Ultra Balls, 10 Max Revives, 10 Max Potions, two Premium Battle Passes, one Incubator, and one Super Incubator as a gift with purchase.
