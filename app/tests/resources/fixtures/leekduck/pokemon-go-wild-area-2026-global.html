Pokémon GO Wild Area 2026: Global

Wild Area
Ticketed Event
Day 1
Saturday, November 14, 2026, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM Local Time
Day 2
Sunday, November 15, 2026, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM Local Time
Share this event

GO Wild Area returns in 2026 with Steel- and Dragon-type Pokémon, the debuts of Gigantamax Duraludon, Dynamax Dialga, and Dynamax Palkia, and powerful mighty Pokémon. Collect GO Safari Balls to help catch these challenging encounters!

Dates: November 14 and 15, 2026
Time: 10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. local time each day
Location: Worldwide

All Trainers can participate in the global event. An optional US$11.99 ticket adds Special Research, increased Shiny chances, extra Incense encounters, and additional bonuses across both days. Purchase a ticket from the Pokémon GO Web Store.

Bonuses

Bonuses for All Trainers

The following bonuses apply from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. local time on both event days.

Chance to get GO Safari Balls from PokéStops and Gyms
Up to 3 free Raid Passes per day from Gyms
Daily Max Particle collection limit increased to 1,600
3 GO Snapshot surprise encounters per day
Party Play lasts for 8 hours
Chance to encounter mighty Pokémon on Routes

Daily Discoveries will be paused from November 9 at 12:01 a.m. through November 15, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. local time.

Additional Ticket Holder Bonuses

These benefits apply from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. local time on both event days. The ticketed Raid Pass and Max Particle limits replace the free limits.

Increased chance to encounter Shiny Pokémon*
Daily Max Particle collection limit increased to 3,200
Increased Max Particles from Power Spots and exploration**
Up to 6 free Raid Passes per day from Gyms
GO Safari Balls instead of Premier Balls for raids and Max Battles***
2× Candy for catching Pokémon
2× Incense duration
Increased chance of 10 km Eggs from PokéStops and Gyms
6 GO Snapshot surprise encounters per day
Lure Modules last for 2 hours

*Shiny encounters are not guaranteed.
**To use the increased exploration Max Particle bonus, first collect all available Max Particles in the Nearby menu. Adventure Sync can help track exploration distance.
***GO Safari Balls replacing Premier Balls after raids and Max Battles is based on how ticketed GO Wild Area events worked in previous years and is not yet confirmed.

Trading Bonuses

Ticket holders receive these benefits from 12:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. local time on both event days.

Up to 6 Special Trades per day
1/2 Stardust cost for trades

Features

Gigantamax Duraludon Debuts

Gigantamax Duraludon makes its Pokémon GO debut at Pokémon GO Wild Area! This larger-than-life Pokémon will appear in six-star Max Battles during the event.

Ticket-holding Trainers can learn more about Gigantamax Duraludon in the event-exclusive Special Research story. Complete it for an encounter with Gigantamax Duraludon with a Special Background!

If you’re lucky, you may encounter a Shiny one!

  • Gigantamax Duraludon

Dynamax Dialga and Dynamax Palkia

Dynamax Dialga and Dynamax Palkia make their Pokémon GO debuts during GO Wild Area 2026. Both can be Shiny!

For Global, Dynamax Dialga appears on Saturday, November 14, and Dynamax Palkia appears on Sunday, November 15.

Private Dynamax Battle Groups

Starting with GO Wild Area 2026, Dynamax Battle lobbies offer Battle to match automatically with other Trainers or Private Group to join an existing group using its group code.

Adventure Effects in Battle Lobbies

Starting with Into the Wild, select Adventure Effects can be activated while waiting in a raid or Max Battle lobby. Use Crowned Sword Zacian’s Behemoth Blade or Crowned Shield Zamazenta’s Behemoth Bash Adventure Effect before a Max Battle to help your group.

Machamp Wearing a Modern Jacket

A new costumed Machamp appears in three-star raids during the event. Lucky Trainers may encounter a Shiny one!

Special Backgrounds

After a successful catch from raids or Max Battles, Machamp wearing a modern jacket, Dynamax Dialga, Dynamax Palkia, Reshiram, Zekrom, Kyurem, and Gigantamax Duraludon have a chance to have a new Special Background.

Backgrounds on Your Trainer Profile

Your buddy Pokémon’s eligible Location Background or Special Background can now appear on your Trainer profile, where friends can see it too. Background support rolls out on the following schedule:

  • November 6, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. local time: GO Wild Area 2026 Location Backgrounds for Sendai and Mexico City.
  • November 9, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. local time: The GO Wild Area 2026 Special Background and all previously released Location Backgrounds.

Friends can see a supported background once its rollout reaches 10:00 a.m. in their own local time. More Special Backgrounds will be supported in the future.

Mighty Latias and Latios

Ticket holders may attract mighty Latias and Latios with Incense, excluding Daily Adventure Incense, on both event days. Both can be Shiny and are quick to flee, so keep GO Safari Balls ready.

GO Safari Balls

GO Safari Balls improve the chance of catching Pokémon, especially mighty Pokémon. All Trainers can obtain them by spinning PokéStops and Gym Photo Discs during event hours.

They can only be used from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. local time on event days and become inaccessible after the event ends.

Pokémon caught in GO Safari Balls can now be sent to Pokémon HOME, where the GO Safari Ball is displayed as a Strange Ball.

Supply Balloons

Ticket holders receive a Supply Balloon every hour during event hours. Each one gives 15 GO Safari Balls.

GO Snapshot, Routes, and Party Play

Take snapshots for surprise encounters with Steel- and Dragon-type Pokémon. Explore Routes for a chance to find mighty Pokémon, and enjoy Party Play sessions lasting eight hours.

Research

Special Research — Ticket Holders

Ticket-holding Trainers can learn more about Gigantamax Duraludon in the event-exclusive Special Research story. Complete it for an encounter with Gigantamax Duraludon with a Special Background!

Claim the event Special Research beginning November 14, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. local time. Choose a Steel-type or Dragon-type path; your choice determines certain rewards and bonuses.

  • Steel Scholar medal: Earn 1 additional Candy XL for catching Steel-type mighty Pokémon (Trainers level 31+) and 1,000 additional Stardust for catching Steel-type mighty Pokémon.
  • Dragon Defender medal: Earn 1 additional Candy XL for catching Dragon-type mighty Pokémon (Trainers level 31+) and 1,000 additional Stardust for catching Dragon-type mighty Pokémon.

Your chosen path’s bonuses begin when you claim its medal and last until November 15, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. local time. Start the research early to make the most of them!

Completing the event Special Research also awards a new avatar pose, or Duraludon stickers if you already have the pose.

Trainers who attended an in-person GO Wild Area event can also receive this research by purchasing a separate Global ticket.
