Bonuses

You can use a Charged TM to help a Shadow Pokémon forget the Charged Attack Frustration
Magnetic Lure Modules will attract different Pokémon during the event, such as Onix, Beldum, Shieldon, and Rookidee
  • Onix
  • Beldum
  • Shieldon
  • Rookidee

Paid Timed Research

The Paid ticket also provides the following bonus.

Features

Pokémon Debuts

The following Pokémon first discovered in the Galar region will make their Pokémon GO debuts!

  • Rookidee
  • Corvisquire
  • Corviknight

You can use 25 Rookidee Candy to evolve Rookidee into Corvisquire and 100 Rookidee Candy to evolve Corvisquire into Corviknight.

GO Battle League

During the event, Rookidee can be encountered in both GO Battle League reward tracks at all ranks, but it will appear more frequently in the premium track.

PokéStop Showcases

Be on the lookout for Showcases at different PokéStops where you can enter event-themed Pokémon!

GO Battle Week: Dual Destiny

GO Battle Week will be happening during the same time. Find more details on GO Battle Week: Dual Destiny here.

Dual Destiny Special Research

Another part of the Seasonal Special Research story will be available to all Trainers beginning at the start of the event.

Complete tasks to earn rewards, including two Fast TMs, two Charged TMs, one Lucky Egg, and more!

You can claim this Special Research at no cost from Tuesday, December 3, at 10:00 a.m. to Tuesday, March 4, 2025, at 9:59 a.m. local time.

There doesn’t seem to be more available when the event starts. The next part was actually expected to be on January 27.
