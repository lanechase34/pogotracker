Psychic Spectacular: Taken Over

Event
Team GO Rocket
Starts
Tuesday, September 16, 2025, 10:00 AM Local Time
Ends
Sunday, September 21, 2025, 8:00 PM Local Time
Share this event

The Psychic Spectacular: Taken Over event arrives, featuring Indeedee’s debut! Face Team GO Rocket, save Shadow Incarnate Forme Tornadus, and enjoy special bonuses throughout the event!

BonusesFeaturesShadowSpawnsEggsRaidsResearchShinySalesGraphic



Advertisement


Bonuses

Additional Candy for Nice Throws or better
1/2 Egg Hatch Distance when Eggs are placed in an Incubator during the event period
Increased chance for Candy XL for Nice Throws or better for Trainers level 31 and up
Lunatone and Solrock will both appear in the wild during the event, regardless of your location
Team GO Rocket will appear more frequently at PokéStops and in balloons* **
You can use a Charged TM to help a Shadow Pokémon forget the Charged Attack Frustration*
Pokémon caught via one-star and three-star Shadow Raids during the event will have a wider variance of Attack, Defense, and HP*

* These bonuses will be active from Tuesday, September 16, at 12:00 a.m. to Sunday, September 21, 2025, at 11:59 p.m. local time.

* * Rocket Balloons are appearing every two hours.

Features

Pokémon Debut

The Emotion Pokémon Indeedee will make its Pokémon GO debut! Also, you’ll be able to hatch Shiny Indeedee—if you’re lucky!

  • Indeedee (Female)
  • Indeedee (Male)

Save Shadow Incarnate Forme Tornadus!

Special Research will be unlocked at the beginning of this event. Progress through it to receive a Super Rocket Radar and chase down Giovanni!

You can claim this Special Research until the end of Pokémon GO: Tales of Transformation on December 2, 2025, at 9:59 a.m. local time.

  • Tornadus (Incarnate)

Shadow

Team GO Rocket Grunts—and their leaders, Sierra, Cliff, and Arlo—are using different Shadow Pokémon.

Team GO Rocket has also turned the following Pokémon into Shadow Pokémon. Defeat Team GO Rocket Grunts to save them!

You can find updated Team GO Rocket lineups here on Leek Duck after the event starts. It will take a couple days for complete lineups.
